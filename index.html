Official framework: Department of Consumer Affairs / Legal Metrology; State Legal Metrology authorities; GATC framework. This is a prototype and does not issue legally valid certificates.

Government Approved Test Centres (GATC)

GATCs are approved under the Legal Metrology (Government Approved Test Centre) Rules for verification of prescribed weights and measures, alongside State Government verification.
